Founder's view

We built the floor.
Now we're building
the pipeline.

Creative Visual Group was started on a simple observation: the custom-fabrication market has plenty of brokers and very few actual floors.

Luxury houses were briefing beautiful work and then watching it pass through three intermediaries before anyone touched a sheet of acrylic. Timelines slipped. Finishes drifted. And when a store needed a change the week of install, there was nobody with authority to say yes.

So we bought the equipment and hired the people. Twenty-five craftspeople in Kenilworth, New Jersey, doing metal, millwork, acrylic, lighting and finish under one roof — with a second component channel in India for the parts where that genuinely lowers cost without touching quality.

The portfolio on the previous page is the result. Chanel, Hermès, Saint Laurent, Loewe, Chloé, Bond No. 9 — vitrines, podiums, towers and full activation environments, most of it standing on the floors of the best beauty halls in the country.

What we have not done well is sell. Every programme in that portfolio arrived through a relationship or a referral. That is the gap this next phase closes.
