About Creative Visual Group

Where design, fabrication
and brand come together.

Creative Visual Group is a luxury visual merchandising and fabrication studio specializing in high end retail environments, window displays, brand activations and custom installations. We bring concepts to life with precision, creativity and a deep understanding of luxury.

Every piece is built in our own factory by a team of about 25 makers, from the first cut to the final finish. Counters, podiums, towers, windows and full brand spaces, made to the standard a luxury floor demands.
